Services

Focused audits, actionable findings

01 / CLAIMS

Scientific claim & evidence audit

Check whether reported conclusions are supported by the study's methods, findings and cited material. Flag overclaims, missing support and internal contradictions.

02 / METHODS

Statistical & methodological review

Examine design logic, controls, assumptions, uncertainty, comparisons, leakage concerns and reproducibility within the agreed review scope.

03 / LITERATURE

State-of-the-art positioning

Examine contribution claims against a documented, bounded literature search, distinguishing plausible differentiation from established novelty.

04 / WRITING

Consistency & scientific clarity

Review argument structure, scientific language, and agreement between abstract, text, tables and figures while preserving the original study's scientific meaning.
